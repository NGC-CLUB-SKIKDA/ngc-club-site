Working Together Towards Innovation

Each department brings a different set of skills and perspectives to New Generation. While their responsibilities differ, they share one common objective: creating an environment where knowledge, creativity, and collaboration lead to meaningful achievements.

By combining scientific research, technological development, communication, and creative design, our departments work together to transform ideas into projects and make innovation a collective experience.

/ THE ADMINISTRATION

Our Team.

The administration is the backbone of New Generation. It turns ideas into authorized, scheduled and well-organized reality, so every department can focus on what it does best.

WHO MAKES IT WORK

PresidentVice PresidentGeneral SecretaryTreasurerLogistics CoordinatorDepartment Heads

Official Paperwork and Authorizations

Requests and permits from the university administration, official letters, partnership agreements and every document the club needs to operate.

Rooms, Equipment and Logistics

Booking classrooms and amphitheaters, preparing equipment and supplies, and making sure every event is set up on time.

Meetings and Minutes (PVs)

Preparing agendas, writing official meeting reports (PVs) and tracking decisions so nothing is forgotten.

Coordination Between Departments

Keeping schedules, workflows and communication aligned across Computer Science, Petrochemistry, Communications, Design and Human Resources.

Budget and Resources

Tracking expenses and contributions, handling routine requests, and making sure funds and equipment are used wisely.

Archives and Documentation

Keeping an organized archive of reports, photos, projects and contracts, so future members can build on what we did.

Rules and Member Support

Turning leadership decisions into clear procedures, applying the club's internal rules fairly, and answering members' questions.

Safety and Compliance

Making sure events and lab activities follow university rules and safety requirements.
